# Manifolds and differential forms

Definitions added in v0.3.0 were checked against the following university lecture notes:

- [Kupers, Lectures on topological manifolds, Definitions 1.1–1.2](https://www.maths.gla.ac.uk/~mpowell/toplectures.pdf): Hausdorff, second-countable, locally Euclidean manifolds; the record uses no boundary.
- [Gualtieri, Differential Geometry, §§5.14, 5.17 and 6.1](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/mgualt/courses/18-1300/docs/18-1300-notes-vb-dr.pdf): alternating tensors, differential forms, wedge product and exterior derivative.
- [MIT 18.952 course notes, §2.3](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.952/spring2013/docs/book.pdf): closed and exact forms; exact implies closed, while a globally closed form need not be exact.

Prerequisite edges are editorial teaching choices informed by these definitions, not claims that the sources publish an ontology. Manifold refinements describe adding smooth or metric structure. Product navigation containment remains outside Core.

## v0.4.0 expansion

- [Hitchin, Differentiable Manifolds, §§2 and 6.5](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/joyce/Nairobi2019/Hitchin-DifferentiableManifolds.pdf): charts, atlases, smooth maps, interior product and Lie derivative.
- [Joyce, Introduction to Differential Geometry, lectures 1–2](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/joyce/Nairobi2019/IDG1%2B2handout.pdf): embedded versus immersed submanifolds; smooth real vector bundles.
- [Cannas da Silva, Lectures on Symplectic Geometry, §§1.3 and 15.1](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.157/2017sp/lecturesonsymplecticgeometryanacannas.pdf): symplectic and complex structures. Forgetting a structure is not a guarantee that it exists on every smooth manifold.
- [Gualtieri, differential forms](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/mgualt/courses/18-1300/docs/18-1300-notes-vb-dr.pdf): pullback and exterior differentiation.
- [Gualtieri, de Rham cohomology](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/mgualt/courses/18-1300/docs/18-1300-notes-dr-cohomology.pdf): de Rham groups, local Poincaré lemma and Stokes with compact support.
- [Joyce, lectures 5–6](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/joyce/Nairobi2019/IDG5%2B6handout.pdf): orientation, integration, and induced boundary orientation.

The manifold record still uses the no-boundary convention. Stokes' Theorem explicitly states its broader with-boundary setting. Closed forms are locally exact in positive degree; no unrestricted global converse is asserted. Lie differentiation of general tensor fields is not made dependent on Cartan's formula for forms.
