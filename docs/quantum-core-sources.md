## Important boundaries

Heisenberg's equation is a scoped equation from the Heisenberg-picture article,
not the uncertainty principle or an alias for the entire picture. The density
matrix identity stays unchanged; its separate consumer annotation gains the
von Neumann equation. Quantum adiabatic following is not thermal isolation.
Open-system reduced evolution is not assumed to be unitary or Markovian.

Approximation methods are siblings, not subtypes of perturbation theory. Quantum
scattering and its cross section are explicitly narrower scopes of broader
canonical Wikipedia articles. The forward elastic amplitude also appears in
multichannel flux conservation. Bound-state tails are not outgoing escape flux.

Fermions and bosons are general quantum particle classes. Chemical potential
is a thermodynamic variable, not a synonym for Fermi energy. Condensation is
not synonymous with superfluidity. Solid-state physics is neither continuum
solid mechanics nor all condensed-matter physics. Brillouin zones here organize
physical crystal wavevectors; no new generic mathematics domain is added.

## Definitions and primary references

- **Quantum Dynamics** (`quantum-dynamics`): Quantum time evolution, including coherent motion, driven transitions, and environmental effects. Excludes: Not synonymous with classical dynamics or any one dynamical equation. [Definition reference](https://quest.ph.utexas.edu/Reviews/Zeno/Zeno.pdf).
- **Heisenberg Equation of Motion** (`heisenberg-equation-of-motion`): The equation within the Heisenberg picture article; one scoped equation identity, not a second picture node. Excludes: Not the uncertainty principle: this governs time evolution rather than bounding measurement spreads. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qm/Quantum/node31.html).
- **Schrödinger Picture** (`schrodinger-picture`): The state-evolving dynamical picture, illustrated by unitary evolution. Excludes: Distinct from the Schrödinger equation itself and from position-space wave mechanics. [Definition reference](https://etneil.github.io/grad_qm_lec_notes/time_pictures.html).
- **Interaction Picture** (`interaction-picture`): Finite-system dynamical representation with H = H₀ + V(t). Excludes: Not a separate physical theory or an assertion that interactions are always weak. [Definition reference](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-74-introductory-quantum-mechanics-ii-spring-2009/5a6dd28ec55f9ea1ff1faf17c847bb2f_MIT5_74s09_lec02.pdf).
- **Ehrenfest Theorem** (`ehrenfest-theorem`): Expectation-value motion, illustrated for a particle in a scalar potential. Excludes: Mean force need not equal force at mean position; arbitrary wavepackets do not follow exact classical trajectories. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qm/lectures/node33.html).
- **Probability Current** (`probability-current`): Quantum probability flow, with the scalar Schrödinger wavefunction as the displayed case. Excludes: Not electric current; the displayed formula excludes vector potentials and spin contributions. [Definition reference](https://etneil.github.io/grad_qm_lec_notes/waves.html).
- **Stationary State** (`stationary-state`): Pure-state stationarity for an undisturbed fixed Hamiltonian. Excludes: Not a motionless particle; stationary probability density can coexist with nonzero current. Stationary mixed states are not the displayed case. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qm/lectures/node34.html).
- **Rabi Oscillation** (`rabi-oscillation`): Driven two-level population dynamics. Excludes: Not the transition rate of an irreversible decay; dephasing and relaxation modify the ideal oscillation. [Definition reference](https://etneil.github.io/grad_qm_lec_notes/ex_rabi.html).
- **Two-state Quantum System** (`two-state-quantum-system`): Two-dimensional quantum systems, exact or effective isolated two-level models. Excludes: Two basis states do not mean only two possible physical states. A two-level truncation needs physical justification. [Definition reference](https://etneil.github.io/grad_qm_lec_notes/two_state.html).
- **Adiabatic Theorem** (`adiabatic-theorem`): Closed-system, nondegenerate gapped adiabatic following; phases are retained. Excludes: Not the thermodynamic meaning of adiabatic. Level crossings and rapid or resonant changes need separate treatment. [Definition reference](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-06-quantum-physics-iii-spring-2018/95ffb7405e75bec4f75564318e173ce0_MIT8_06S18ch6.pdf).
- **Geometric Phase** (`geometric-phase`): Quantum geometric phase, with the cyclic adiabatic Berry phase as a representative formula. Excludes: The geometric phase is not the dynamical phase; the displayed adiabatic expression does not cover every generalization. [Definition reference](https://etneil.github.io/grad_qm_lec_notes/app_berry_phase.html).
- **Probability Amplitude** (`probability-amplitude`): Quantum amplitudes for outcomes and transitions; discrete normalized-state example. Excludes: Not a probability itself. Transition amplitudes are an application, not a separate synonym for every amplitude. [Definition reference](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/III_01.html).
- **Bloch Sphere** (`bloch-sphere`): Representation of normalized two-level pure and mixed states. Excludes: Not a physical orbit or ordinary spatial position; the sphere surface alone represents pure states. [Definition reference](https://etneil.github.io/grad_qm_lec_notes/density.html).
- **Open Quantum System** (`open-quantum-system`): System–environment quantum dynamics and reduced-state descriptions. Excludes: Not every open-system process is Markovian, thermal, irreversible, or captured by a time-independent master equation. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/aqm/aqmnine.pdf).
- **Quantum Decoherence** (`quantum-decoherence`): Environment-induced decoherence; a pure-dephasing channel illustrates loss of off-diagonal coherence. Excludes: Does not by itself select a unique measurement outcome or prove fundamental wavefunction collapse. Coherence depends on the physical basis. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/aqm/aqmnine.pdf).
- **Quantum Zeno Effect** (`quantum-zeno-effect`): Quantum evolution under repeated monitoring, illustrated with ideal survival projections. Excludes: No conscious observer is required. Arbitrary monitoring need not suppress transitions; other regimes can accelerate them. [Definition reference](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/22-51-quantum-theory-of-radiation-interactions-fall-2012/693972cea9cf9da5e4e9e86fbc72c9e7_MIT22_51F12_Notes.pdf).
- **Quantum Perturbation Theory** (`quantum-perturbation-theory`): Stationary and time-dependent quantum perturbation methods; the displayed formula is the nondegenerate stationary first-order case. Excludes: Not identical to a particular energy-correction formula; degenerate levels require a separate treatment. [Definition reference](https://interactivetextbooks.tudelft.nl/introduction-to-quantum-mechanics/content/perturbationtheory.html).
- **Fermi's Golden Rule** (`fermi-golden-rule`): Continuum transition rates at leading nonzero order in a weak perturbation. Excludes: Not an exact finite-time transition probability or a rule for strong coherent driving. [Definition reference](https://interactivetextbooks.tudelft.nl/introduction-to-quantum-mechanics/content/perturbationtheory.html).
- **Quantum Variational Method** (`quantum-variational-method`): Ground-state Rayleigh-quotient minimization and its energy upper bound. Excludes: Distinct from classical stationary-action principles; approximate orthogonality alone does not guarantee an excited-state upper bound. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node127.html).
- **WKB Approximation** (`wkb-approximation`): Introductory one-dimensional semiclassical quantum approximation; the formula gives a leading tunnelling exponential. Excludes: Not exact near turning points; not synonymous with all perturbation theory. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node49.html).
- **Bound State** (`bound-state`): Bound states in nonrelativistic potential problems, including confining models and localized attractive wells. Excludes: Not a metastable resonance or an arbitrary localized wavepacket; negative energy is meaningful only relative to the escape threshold. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qm/qmhtml/S2.html).
- **Particle in a Box** (`particle-in-a-box`): Ideal hard-wall confinement; the formula is the one-dimensional interval model. Excludes: Not a finite-depth well, periodic boundary conditions, or the model's energy formula alone. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node47.html).
- **Quantum Harmonic Oscillator** (`quantum-harmonic-oscillator`): One-dimensional undamped, undriven quadratic quantum oscillator. Excludes: Distinct from the classical oscillator and from a general bosonic Fock-space construction; anharmonic interactions are excluded. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node53.html).
- **Quantum Scattering** (`quantum-scattering`): Quantum scattering, initially nonrelativistic potential scattering. The directly resolved canonical Wikipedia article Scattering is broader; its lead and exact-revision quantum-theory discussion were reviewed. Excludes: Excludes classical wave and particle scattering as separate subjects. This scoped concept is not a binding to the Scattering theory redirect or its section. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/aqm/aqmten.pdf).
- **Born Approximation** (`born-approximation`): First Born approximation for nonrelativistic elastic scattering from a localized potential. Excludes: Not the Born probability rule or a generally exact formula; strong scattering and untreated long-range Coulomb asymptotics lie outside this form. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node133.html).
- **Scattering Amplitude** (`scattering-amplitude`): Quantum scattering amplitudes, introduced through the nonrelativistic three-dimensional elastic channel. Multichannel problems retain a forward elastic amplitude alongside amplitudes for other accessible channels. Excludes: Not a scattering probability; relativistic invariant-amplitude conventions and long-range modifications require separate normalization. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node132.html).
- **Scattering Cross Section** (`scattering-cross-section`): Operational and angular cross sections in quantum scattering. The canonical Cross section (physics) article also covers other collision and absorption processes. Excludes: Not necessarily the target's geometric area; the displayed angular relation is restricted to a single elastic channel. [Definition reference](https://quantummechanics.ucsd.edu/ph130a/130_notes/node441.html).
- **Scattering Matrix** (`scattering-matrix`): Quantum scattering operator and its channel representation, introduced through potential scattering. Excludes: Not a classical transfer matrix; an elastic subblock need not be unitary when omitted channels carry probability. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/aqm/aqmten.pdf).
- **Optical Theorem** (`optical-theorem`): Quantum potential-scattering optical theorem and its total-channel interpretation. Excludes: Not restricted to visible light; it is not a relation between the total cross section and the amplitude's absolute square. [Definition reference](https://quantummechanics.ucsd.edu/ph130a/130_notes/node441.html).
- **Quantum Tunnelling** (`quantum-tunnelling`): Nonrelativistic barrier transmission and its wave-mechanical explanation. Excludes: Not temporary borrowing of energy or ordinary over-barrier motion; evanescent penetration alone does not imply escape from a true bound state. [Definition reference](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/7-6-the-quantum-tunneling-of-particles-through-potential-barriers).
- **Rectangular Potential Barrier** (`rectangular-potential-barrier`): One-dimensional finite-width, finite-height rectangular barrier; exact sub-barrier transmission is displayed. Excludes: Not an arbitrary potential barrier, a potential step of infinite width, or the tunnelling phenomenon itself. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node48.html).
- **Finite Potential Well** (`finite-potential-well`): The one-dimensional finite square-well model bound by two finite potential steps. Excludes: Not an infinite well or a finite-width barrier. Exterior evanescent tails of bound states do not describe a steady outgoing escape flux. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node52.html).
- **Quantum Statistical Mechanics** (`quantum-statistical-mechanics`): Quantum ensembles and introductory equilibrium applications, including identical-particle statistics. Excludes: Not a synonym for either single-particle quantum mechanics or only the Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac distributions. [Definition reference](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/8d8391a5b4d128c30b35fd1a1527df88_34lmLIYpkYQ.pdf).
- **Fermion** (`fermion`): Elementary and composite fermions in the usual three-dimensional spin-statistics setting. Excludes: Not limited to electrons; not the Fermi-gas model. Two-dimensional anyonic statistics are outside this scope. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node93.html).
- **Boson** (`boson`): Elementary and composite bosons under the usual spin-statistics assumptions. Excludes: Not only force carriers; not a Bose gas or a condensate. Anyons and effective quasiparticle exceptions need separate treatment. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node93.html).
- **Fermi–Dirac Statistics** (`fermi-dirac-statistics`): Equilibrium state counting and mean occupations of ideal fermions. Excludes: The occupation function is not a probability density over energies, and it is not itself the Fermi-gas model. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node96.html).
- **Bose–Einstein Statistics** (`bose-einstein-statistics`): Equilibrium bosonic state counting and the ideal-particle mean occupation law. Excludes: Not the Bose-gas model or Bose–Einstein condensation itself; the occupation is not a normalized energy probability density. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node98.html).
- **Chemical Potential** (`chemical-potential`): Equilibrium chemical potential, expressed here as energy per particle for a single component. Excludes: Not generally Fermi energy. Per-mole conventions and electrochemical potentials require explicit units and field contributions. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/statphys/statmechhtml/S1.html).
- **Fermi Energy** (`fermi-energy`): Zero-temperature Fermi energy, with the bottom of the one-particle spectrum used as the energy reference. Excludes: Not an unqualified alias for Fermi level or the temperature-dependent chemical potential; finite-temperature occupations are distinct. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node106.html).
- **Bose–Einstein Condensate** (`bose-einstein-condensate`): Equilibrium Bose–Einstein condensation and its homogeneous ideal-gas benchmark. Excludes: Not synonymous with superfluidity, superconductivity, or every cold Bose gas; interacting and trapped condensates need additional models. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node111.html).
- **Fermi Gas** (`fermi-gas`): The ideal fermionic gas and its use as a first approximation to systems such as conduction electrons. Excludes: Not a Fermi liquid, an interacting electron theory, or the Fermi–Dirac occupation formula itself. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node106.html).
- **Bose Gas** (`bose-gas`): The ideal Bose gas, including its normal phase and the ideal-gas condensation limit. Excludes: Not synonymous with a condensate; interactions, trap geometry, and dimensionality require explicit extensions. [Definition reference](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/statphys/statmechhtml/S3.html).
- **Density of States** (`density-of-states`): Physical state counting by energy, especially single-particle states and vibrational modes; total-system and per-volume conventions are distinguished. Excludes: Not a normalized probability density or an occupied-particle density. Many-body and local densities of states require their own specifications. [Definition reference](https://tsymbal.unl.edu/sites/unl.edu.cas.physics.tsymbal/files/media/file/Section%2007_Free_Electron_Model.pdf).
- **Black-Body Radiation** (`black-body-radiation`): The thermal radiation field of an ideal black body or equilibrium cavity. Excludes: Not every thermal emitter is a black body, and nonthermal light is excluded. Planck's law is the separate spectral equation. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node104.html).
- **Planck's Law** (`planck-law`): Planck's thermal spectrum, represented here as energy density per angular-frequency interval in vacuum. Excludes: Not the phenomenon of black-body radiation itself. Spectral radiance, energy density, and frequency or wavelength forms have different prefactors and Jacobians. [Definition reference](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/sm1/Thermalhtml/node103.html).
- **Phonon** (`phonon`): Quantized collective vibrations, with harmonic-crystal acoustic and optical modes as the introductory realization. Excludes: Not the motion of one isolated atom; not restricted to linear acoustic dispersion. Anharmonic interactions and noncrystalline extensions require additional treatment. [Definition reference](https://tsymbal.unl.edu/sites/unl.edu.cas.physics.tsymbal/files/media/file/Section%2005_Lattice_Vibrations.pdf).
- **Bloch's Theorem** (`bloch-theorem`): Bloch eigenstates of periodic one-particle or effective independent-particle Hamiltonians. Excludes: Not every arbitrary superposition has a single Bloch wavevector. Nonperiodic disorder and general magnetic-translation problems are outside this elementary form. [Definition reference](https://davidtong.org/pdfs/teaching/solid-state-physics/solidstate2.pdf).
- **Electronic Band Structure** (`electronic-band-structure`): Electronic bands in solids, approached through periodic-crystal independent-particle or effective-potential theory. Excludes: Not an isolated atom's electron configuration, and not the band gap alone. Strong correlation and disorder require extensions. [Definition reference](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-091sc-introduction-to-solid-state-chemistry-fall-2010/pages/electronic-materials/13-band-theory-of-solids/).
- **Brillouin Zone** (`brillouin-zone`): Physical crystal-wavevector organization, emphasizing the first zone while distinguishing higher-zone constructions. Excludes: Not a real-space unit cell or an irreducible symmetry wedge. Reciprocal-lattice mathematics is supporting structure, not a new mathematics field here. [Definition reference](https://tsymbal.unl.edu/sites/unl.edu.cas.physics.tsymbal/files/media/file/Section%2002_X-ray_Diffraction.pdf).
- **Solid-State Physics** (`solid-state-physics`): Solid-state physics, with this introductory branch emphasizing periodic electronic states and quantized vibrations. Excludes: Not synonymous with continuum solid mechanics or with all condensed-matter physics; the broader field also includes noncrystalline solids. [Definition reference](https://davidtong.org/teaching/solid-state-physics/).
